## 📋 BACKGROUND INFORMATION

## Use Case Overview
**Goal:** Enable builders to create and manage custom system prompts for personalized AI context  
**Problem:** Generic AI responses don't match organizational needs and communication styles  
**Solution:** User-defined custom system prompts with management interface and team collaboration

## Business Value
- **Organizational Customization:** Each company defines AI behavior according to their standards
- **Knowledge Preservation:** Capture institutional knowledge in reusable prompt templates
- **Consistency:** Standardized AI responses aligned with company communication styles
- **Flexibility:** Adapt AI context as business needs evolve

## User Story
**As a** dashboard builder  
**I want** to create custom system prompts  
**So that** I can tailor AI behavior to my organization's specific needs and communication style

## Acceptance Criteria
- ✅ Custom prompt creation with name, description, and content fields
- ✅ Prompt management interface (edit, remove, organize)
- ✅ Quick creation tools with suggestion chips and templates
- ✅ Team collaboration features for sharing standardized prompts

### Key Value Propositions
- **User-Defined Context**: Builders create custom prompts tailored to their specific needs
- **Prompt Management**: Edit, remove, and organize custom system prompts
- **Quick Creation Tools**: Suggestion chips and templates for rapid prompt development
- **Team Collaboration**: Share and standardize custom prompts across organizations
